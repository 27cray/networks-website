Home Network Equipment

Router

A Router is a device that connects computers to the internet. It directs digital traffic directing packets to their destination through the best path. A router creates a local network allowing you to do things wirelessly. Routers have firewalls that protect you local Network from outside threats. This also distributes ethernet and Wi-FI to your devices.

Modem

A modem acts as a bridge between your network and your internet service provider. Connecting your home to the internet. if you connect a modem and a router together like my family does the modem provides the internet that the router distributes.
